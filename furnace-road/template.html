How to drive

WheelPut a finger on the steering wheel and twist it like a real one. Clockwise turns right, and the Jeep's front wheels follow exactly how far you turn. Let go and it centres itself. Tap the hub to honk. GasHold the tall pedal. It's a 4-speed automatic that shifts on its own; the needle and tach show what the engine is doing. BrakeHold the wide pedal. Keep holding at a standstill and it drops into reverse; press gas to go forward again. FuelThe tank runs about four highway miles. Blue GAS signs warn you half a mile out. Pull up beside a pump under the canopy, stop, and tap FILL UP. WaterThe heat dries you out, faster in the afternoon. Stop by the water table at any station and tap GRAB WATER (4 bottles max), then tap DRINK when the drop runs low. Off-roadSand and gravel have less grip and more drag. Slow down before the shoulder or the rear end steps out.

Run dry and you'll coast to a stop; a tow truck takes you to the next station. Keyboard: W/S gas & brake, A/D steer, H horn, C camera, P pause.
