How to play

StickTouch anywhere on the left half and drag. Up is toward the hoop. ShootHold to rise, release when the bar hits the green band at the top of your jump. Early falls short, late goes long. Layup · DunkThe shoot button changes inside 3 m. Attack the rim at speed (sprint helps) and it becomes DUNK. FadePull the stick away from the hoop while you shoot. Harder to time, much harder to contest. CrossSwitches hands with a burst. Stick away from the hoop makes it a step-back; sprinting makes it a spin. Time it when the defender reaches. SprintHold. Uses the amber stamina bar. Block · StealOn defense the buttons flip. Jump into the shot to block, swipe when you're close and the ball is on your side. Pass · CallIn practice, pass to your rebounder or call for the ball.

1 on 1 rules: check ball at the top of the key, 14-second shot clock, and after a defensive rebound or steal you must take it back past the arc before you can score. Reach-ins are fouls, goaltending counts. Keyboard: WASD, J shoot, K pass/steal, L cross, Shift sprint, P pause.
